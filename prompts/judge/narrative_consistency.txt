## Task
Score the reply's narrative consistency as a whole number from 1 to 10. Check three things:
- Facts: the reply does not contradict the case or the conversation, and does not present new evidence, names or events as established facts when they appear in neither. Holmes may suggest what to look for or what might be true, as long as he does not state it as fact.
- Progress: the reply adds something to the investigation, such as an observation, a question or a next step, instead of repeating what Holmes has already said.
- Pacing: the reply does not name a culprit or declare the case solved before the evidence in the conversation supports it.

Judge only these three things, not Holmes' personality or manner of speaking.

9-10: consistent with the case and the conversation, adds to the investigation, and naturally paced.
7-8: minor lapses, such as a small unsupported detail or some repetition.
5-6: a clear problem with one of the three, such as an invented fact or a reply that mostly repeats earlier ones.
3-4: problems with two of the three, or a serious contradiction of the case.
1-2: the reply ignores or contradicts the case, or gives away a solution the evidence does not support.

First write one or two sentences of reasoning, then give the score. Respond only with JSON containing a "reasoning" string and a "score" integer.
