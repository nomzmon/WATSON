## Criteria
Judge the conversation so far together with the visitor's new message. Score each criterion with a whole number from 1 to 10. A low score means Holmes' next reply needs guidance on that criterion.

CST (Conversation State Tracking): Does the dialogue remain within the appropriate stage of the investigation? Stages move from case introduction to evidence gathering, suspect analysis, deduction and case resolution. Give low scores when the conversation is advancing too quickly, skipping stages, or revisiting completed stages without reason.

NP (Narrative Progression): Is the conversation developing toward solving the case at a natural pace? Observations, evidence and deductions should be introduced gradually rather than conclusions being revealed at once. Give low scores when the visitor is pushing for the solution too early or when the investigation has stalled.

CC (Context Continuity): Is previously established information preserved? Earlier evidence, suspects, observations and deductions should be carried forward without contradiction or needless repetition. Give low scores when established facts are being forgotten, contradicted or ignored.

TBC (Topic Boundary Control): Is the conversation focused on the current investigation? Minor deviations are acceptable. Give low scores for significant topic shifts that interrupt the investigation.

## Stage
Also give the stage the investigation has now reached, using exactly one of: introduction, evidence, suspects, deduction, resolution.

## Score guide (applies to every criterion)
9-10: fully satisfies the criterion.
7-8: satisfies the criterion, with at most negligible issues.
5-6: minor issues that the next reply should correct.
3-4: significant problems that the next reply must address.
1-2: the conversation clearly fails the criterion.

For each criterion, first write one or two sentences of reasoning, then give the score. Respond only with JSON containing the keys CST, NP, CC and TBC, each holding a "reasoning" string and a "score" integer, and a key "stage" holding the stage.
